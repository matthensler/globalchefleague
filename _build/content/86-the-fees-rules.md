### 1. Sweepstakes Overview

The Global Chef League Sweepstakes (“Sweepstakes”) is sponsored by Playbook Group, parent company of Global Chef League (“Sponsor”).

The Sweepstakes offers eligible participants the opportunity to win complimentary Global Chef League memberships.

A total of eighty-six (86) winners will be selected:

- Ten (10) Lifetime Global Chef League Membership winners.
- Seventy-six (76) One-Year Global Chef League Membership winners.

By participating, entrants agree to comply with these Official Rules and the decisions of Sponsor regarding Sweepstakes administration, subject to applicable law.

### 2. Eligibility

The Sweepstakes is open to legal residents of the fifty (50) United States and the District of Columbia who are eighteen (18) years of age or older at the time of entry.

No professional culinary experience, employment in the restaurant industry, or existing Global Chef League membership is required.

Employees, officers, directors, contractors, and representatives of Sponsor, its affiliated companies, and agencies involved in administering the Sweepstakes, together with their immediate family members and household members, are ineligible.

The Sweepstakes is subject to all applicable federal, state, and local laws and regulations and is void where prohibited.

### 3. Sweepstakes Entry Period

The Sweepstakes begins on **Sunday, November 15, 2026, at 12:00:01 AM Pacific Time** and ends on **Monday, December 14, 2026, at 11:59:59 PM Pacific Time** (“Sweepstakes Period”).

All entries, including bonus entries, must be received during the Sweepstakes Period to be eligible.

Sponsor's designated timekeeping system will serve as the official clock.

### 4. How to Enter

To enter the Sweepstakes, eligible individuals must:

1. Visit https://globalchefleague.com/sweepstakes.
2. Complete the official Sweepstakes registration form.
3. Provide their name, valid email address, and any other required eligibility information.
4. Confirm they are at least eighteen (18) years old and agree to these Official Rules.
5. Submit their registration during the Sweepstakes Period.

Each eligible participant may complete one (1) initial registration.

**Bonus Entries**

Following initial registration, participants may earn additional Sweepstakes entries by completing qualifying activities offered through the official Sweepstakes platform.

Qualifying activities may include:

- Visiting designated Global Chef League website pages.
- Completing designated surveys or promotional activities.
- Exploring designated Global Chef League membership information.
- Other eligible activities identified within the Sweepstakes platform.

The number of bonus entries awarded for each activity and any per-action limits will be disclosed in the Sweepstakes platform.

Certain activities may be completed only once, while others may be repeatable, as indicated in the platform.

There is no overall entry limit unless otherwise specified in the Sweepstakes platform.

Where required by applicable law, participants will be offered a free alternative method to obtain equivalent bonus entries without completing promotional activities. Instructions and applicable limits will be disclosed in the Sweepstakes platform.

**Social Media Restrictions**

Participants are not required or incentivized to share, repost, tag other individuals, or otherwise publicize the Sweepstakes through Facebook or Instagram.

Sharing, reposting, or tagging others on Meta platforms will not award additional Sweepstakes entries.

**No Purchase Necessary**

No purchase, payment, membership fee, merchandise purchase, or charitable donation is required to enter or win.

Purchasing Global Chef League merchandise, joining Global Chef League as a paying member, or making a donation to Chef Relief will not increase the chances of winning.

Sponsor reserves the right to verify completion of qualifying activities and disqualify fraudulent, automated, duplicate, or otherwise invalid entries.

### 5. Sweepstakes Prizes

A total of eighty-six (86) prizes will be awarded.

**Grand Prizes — 10 Winners**

Ten (10) winners will each receive one (1) complimentary Lifetime Global Chef League Membership.

A Lifetime Membership remains valid for the lifetime of the individual winner, provided the Global Chef League membership program continues to operate and the winner remains in compliance with applicable membership terms.

A Lifetime Membership does not guarantee the perpetual operation of Global Chef League or any particular membership benefit.

**Additional Prizes — 76 Winners**

Seventy-six (76) winners will each receive one (1) complimentary One-Year Global Chef League Membership.

Each One-Year Membership will remain valid for twelve (12) consecutive months beginning upon activation.

One-Year Memberships will not automatically renew as paid memberships without the winner's affirmative authorization.

**General Prize Conditions**

All memberships are subject to Global Chef League membership terms and conditions.

Prizes are nontransferable and cannot be exchanged for cash.

Sponsor reserves the right to substitute a prize of equal or greater value if a stated prize becomes unavailable.

### 6. Winner Selection and Odds

The official random drawing will take place on or about **Tuesday, December 15, 2026**.

Winners will be selected through random drawings conducted by Sponsor or its designated Sweepstakes administrator.

Winners will be selected in the following order:

1. Ten (10) eligible participants will be randomly selected to receive Lifetime Global Chef League Memberships.
2. All entries associated with those ten winners will be removed from subsequent drawings.
3. Seventy-six (76) additional eligible participants will be randomly selected to receive One-Year Global Chef League Memberships.

Each participant may win a maximum of one (1) prize.

**Odds of Winning**

The odds of winning depend on the total number of eligible entries received and the number of valid entries earned by each participant.

Participants who earn additional eligible entries will have proportionately greater chances of winning than participants with fewer entries.

Sponsor's decisions regarding eligibility, entry validation, and winner selection are final, subject to applicable law.
