Frequently Asked Questions.

Still have questions? Email chefbattle@hoptoit.events.

Can I promote my culinary services?

Yes. Your membership includes a public League Chef profile where you can showcase your experience, services, rates, and contact information.

Does Global Chef League take a commission on bookings?

No. Customers contact you directly, and you manage your own bookings and payments.

Will I receive guaranteed bookings?

No. Your profile makes it easier for potential customers to discover you, but bookings are not guaranteed.

Who can become a League Chef?

Membership is open to professional chefs, private chefs, caterers, culinary students, and aspiring chefs. Individual competitions may have additional eligibility requirements.

What goes on my profile?

Your photo, a short bio, the services you offer with starting rates, your culinary specialties, a photo gallery, and how customers can reach you: email, phone, website, booking link and social media.

Do I need to be a League Chef to compete in Chef Battle?

Yes. Active Global Chef League membership is required to compete in official Chef Battle competitions.

Is there an additional competition entry fee?

No. Active League Chefs do not pay additional Chef Battle competition entry fees. Travel, accommodations, and personal expenses are not included.

Does membership renew automatically?

Yes. Membership renews annually unless canceled before the next renewal date.

Can I cancel my membership?

Yes. You can cancel future renewals through the subscription management link provided. Your membership remains active through the paid term, subject to the Membership Terms.
