Our Mission

Care, Built Into the League.

Chefs spend their careers feeding their communities, often in an industry with thin margins and little safety net. One unexpected setback can put everything they've built at risk.

Chef Relief is Global Chef League's charitable program. It exists so that when a chef hits a hard moment, the culinary community can step in and help them get through it.

What We Do

How Chef Relief Helps.

01
Emergency financial assistance

Direct support for chefs hit by an unexpected hardship, when they need it most.

02
Essential expenses covered

Help with the essential costs that can't wait while a chef gets back on their feet.

03
Back to the kitchen

The goal is stability, so chefs can keep cooking and continue the careers they've built.

For Chefs

Need Help?

If you're a chef facing an unexpected hardship, such as an illness or injury, a sudden loss of work, or a family emergency, we want to hear from you.
