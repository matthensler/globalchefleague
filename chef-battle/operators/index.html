How It Works

We've Built the Playbook. You Bring It to Life.

You don't need to create an event from scratch or figure out how Chef Battle should work. We've built the brand, competition format and event playbook. You follow the framework and execute it in your local market.

01
One per market

One Operator Per City

We're selecting one approved Chef Battle Local Operator for each market, giving you the exclusive opportunity to lead Chef Battle in your community.

02
No event financing

We Fund Approved Event Expenses

Hop To It funds the approved event budget so you don't have to finance the event yourself. You focus on execution, not capital.

03
Your role

Follow the Playbook + Execute Locally

We provide the framework. You use your local relationships to recruit chefs, build sponsor partnerships, promote the event and lead on-the-ground execution.

04
Your upside

Earn 30% of Event Profit

Hop To It manages event revenue and approved expenses. You receive 30% of the final Event Profit from every Chef Battle event you operate.

The Partnership

You're Local. You're Not Alone.

You bring the local knowledge and relationships. We bring everything else.

What You Bring
  • Knowledge of your local food and restaurant community
  • Relationships with chefs, venues and local businesses
  • Ability to recruit local sponsors and partners
  • Local marketing and ticket sales support
  • Event-day coordination and on-the-ground execution
  • The drive to make it happen
What We Provide
  • The Chef Battle brand and competition format
  • Operator onboarding and ongoing support
  • Event planning resources and templates
  • Brand and creative resources
  • Sponsorship materials
  • Ticketing infrastructure
  • Approved event funding
  • Financial reconciliation and profit-sharing
Who We're Looking For

You Don't Need to Be a Professional Event Producer.

You might work in events, hospitality, food and beverage, sales, marketing or run your own business. Or you may simply know your city, love bringing people together and have the drive to make things happen.

Entrepreneurial

You see opportunities and go after them.

Connected

You know your local food and business community.

Organized

Relationship-Driven

You build trust and get people on board.

Community-Minded

You care about the people and places in your city.
