The Model

How It Works.

01
One per city

Exclusive Territory

We're selecting one approved Chef Battle International Operator per market. You become the person leading Chef Battle in your community.

02
Your investment

You Fund Your Market

As an International Operator, you fund approved event expenses and manage local event finances. You keep more control, and take more ownership, of your market.

03
Your role

You Lead and Market Locally

You use your knowledge and relationships to recruit chefs, connect with venues, build sponsorships, promote the event and drive ticket sales.

04
Your upside

You Share in the Profit

International Operators receive 60% of final Event Profit from the Chef Battle events they operate. Hop To It retains 40%.

The Experience

More Than a Local Competition.

Every Chef Battle celebrates the people behind the local food scene. Guests see chefs outside their restaurants, experience the competition up close, taste their creations and cheer on their favorites. Local winners become City Champions, with the chance to advance through the Chef Battle series and represent their community on a larger stage.

It starts locally. But the competition doesn't have to end there.
