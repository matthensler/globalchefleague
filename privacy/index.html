Privacy Policy.

Effective Date: November 4, 2026

Global Chef League ("GCL," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information through our website, membership program, directory, and related activities.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you register, purchase a membership, submit a chef profile, or communicate with us:

  • Name and contact information, including email address and phone number.
  • City, state, country, and business location.
  • Professional experience, culinary specialties, biographies, and credentials.
  • Photographs, social media profiles, and website links.
  • Membership status, transaction history, and payment-related records.
  • Competition applications and participation information.
  • Website usage information, including IP addresses, browser information, cookies, and analytics data.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create and manage memberships.
  • Process membership payments.
  • Build and maintain chef directory profiles.
  • Communicate membership benefits, program updates, and competition opportunities.
  • Coordinate Chef Battle events and participant selection.
  • Promote members and related programs.
  • Improve our website, services, and member experience.
  • Prevent fraud and comply with legal obligations.

Where required, we will obtain consent before sending promotional communications.

3. Public Chef Directory

Global Chef League may display member-submitted professional information in a publicly accessible directory.

This may include names, photographs, professional biographies, specialties, business locations, websites, social media links, and contact details selected for publication.

Members should not submit sensitive personal information or private contact information they do not want publicly displayed.

Members may request changes or removal of their directory information by contacting GCL.

4. Payment Processing

Membership payments may be processed through Stripe or other authorized payment providers.

GCL does not store complete payment card numbers on its own servers.

Payment providers process information according to their own privacy policies and security practices.

5. Sharing Information

We may share information with:

  • Payment processors and technology service providers.
  • Authorized Chef Battle organizers and local operators when necessary to coordinate competition opportunities.
  • Service providers assisting with website hosting, email communications, analytics, and member management.
  • Legal or regulatory authorities when required by law.

We do not sell member contact lists to third-party advertisers.

Information submitted for public directory display may be accessible to website visitors.

6. Marketing Communications

Members may receive emails regarding membership updates, available competitions, directory features, and related GCL programs.

Promotional emails will include an unsubscribe option where required by law.

Certain administrative or transactional communications may continue while membership remains active.

7. Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve functionality, understand website traffic, and measure marketing effectiveness.

Visitors may be able to manage certain cookie preferences through their browser or available website privacy controls.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information.

However, no online platform or electronic transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to administer memberships, provide services, maintain required business records, resolve disputes, and comply with legal obligations.

Members may request deletion of eligible personal information, subject to applicable legal and operational retention requirements.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing activities.

You may contact us through globalchefleague.com to submit a privacy request.

We will review and respond to requests in accordance with applicable law.

11. International Members

Global Chef League may accept members from countries outside the United States.

Personal information may be processed or stored in the United States or other locations where our service providers operate.

Where required, we will apply appropriate safeguards to international data transfers.

12. Children's Privacy

Global Chef League membership is intended for individuals aged 18 and older.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18 through our membership registration program.

13. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to external websites, social media platforms, and third-party services.

GCL is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those external websites.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our services, technology, or legal obligations.

The most current version will be published on our website with an updated effective date.

15. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact Global Chef League through the contact information provided at globalchefleague.com.
